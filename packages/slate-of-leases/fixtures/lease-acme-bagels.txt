SHOPPING CENTER LEASE
(fictional sample for testing; not a real agreement)

This Lease is made as of January 15, 2023, between Harbor Point Owner LLC ("Landlord") and Acme Bagels LLC ("Tenant").

ARTICLE 1. BASIC TERMS

1.1 Premises. Suite 100 of Harbor Point Plaza, containing approximately 2,000 rentable square feet.

1.2 Term. The term of this Lease is five (5) years, beginning on the Commencement Date of March 1, 2023 and ending on the Expiration Date of February 29, 2028.

1.3 Permitted Use. Bagel bakery and cafe, and no other use.

--- Page 5 ---

ARTICLE 4. RENT

4.1 Base Rent. Tenant shall pay monthly Base Rent of $4,000.00 in advance on the first day of each month.

4.2 Rent Adjustments. On each anniversary of the Commencement Date, monthly Base Rent shall increase by three percent (3%) over the Base Rent then in effect.

4.3 Security Deposit. Upon signing, Tenant shall deposit with Landlord a security deposit of $8,000.00.

--- Page 7 ---

ARTICLE 5. PERCENTAGE RENT

5.1 Tenant shall pay as Percentage Rent six percent (6%) of Gross Sales in excess of the natural breakpoint for each Lease Year. Tenant shall deliver an annual statement of Gross Sales within sixty (60) days after the end of each Lease Year.

--- Page 9 ---

ARTICLE 6. OPERATING EXPENSES

6.3 Tenant shall pay its Pro Rata Share of Operating Expenses, being five percent (5%), plus an administrative fee of ten percent (10%) of such share. Variable Operating Expenses shall be grossed up to reflect ninety-five percent (95%) occupancy. Tenant's share of Controllable Operating Expenses shall not increase by more than five percent (5%) per year on a cumulative basis over the 2023 calendar year.

--- Page 12 ---

ARTICLE 9. INSURANCE

9.1 Tenant shall carry commercial general liability insurance with limits of not less than $1,000,000 per occurrence and $2,000,000 in the aggregate, naming Landlord as an additional insured.

ARTICLE 14. OPTION TO EXTEND

14.1 Tenant shall have one (1) option to extend the Term for five (5) years, exercisable by written notice given at least six (6) months before the Expiration Date. Base Rent for the extension shall be 95% of fair market rent.

ARTICLE 20. NOTICES

20.1 Notices to Tenant shall be sent to: Acme Bagels LLC, 1 Fictional Rd, Springfield.
